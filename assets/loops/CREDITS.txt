Local film loops — personal use only, never committed.

The clips this folder is generated from are stills and scenes from Studio
Ghibli films (© Studio Ghibli), Makoto Shinkai films (© CoMix Wave Films),
and various AI-generated homages downloaded from social video sites. They
are used here as personal desktop ambience for the machine's owner and are
excluded from the public repository by .gitignore.
